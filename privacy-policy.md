# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 06/05/2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to STAR BLOXX.

We are committed to protecting user privacy, especially for children. This game is designed for a general audience, including children under 13.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information such as real names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

However, the game may collect:

* A username chosen by the player (not required to be real)
* Game progress and scores (for leaderboard functionality)
* Device information (such as device type and OS)
* General location (country level only)
* App usage data and crash reports

---

## 3. Leaderboard & Firebase

The game uses Firebase to store leaderboard data.

* Usernames and scores are stored in a database
* This data is used only to display leaderboard rankings
* We do not link usernames to real-world identities

Users should avoid using real names or personal information in usernames.

---

## 4. Advertising

This game uses:

* Google AdMob
* Unity Ads

For child users:

* Only non-personalized ads are shown
* No behavioral tracking is used

These services may collect limited technical data to display ads.

More info:

* Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
* Unity: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

---

## 5. How We Use Information

We use data to:

* Save game progress and leaderboard scores
* Improve gameplay experience
* Display non-personalized ads
* Fix bugs and improve performance

---

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

Data may be processed by third-party services (Firebase, AdMob, Unity Ads) strictly for app functionality.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

We comply with:

* COPPA
* GDPR-K

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we discover such data has been collected, we will delete it.

---

## 8. Parental Rights

Parents can contact us to:

* Request deletion of leaderboard data
* Ask questions about data usage

---

## 9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored data, but no system is completely secure.

---

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here.
